GenomeOS

🧭Start here

🧬
GenomeOS is an executable model of biology.DNA is the source, BioLang is the language you write biology in, and the runtime plays it forward through time. Every rule carries its evidence and a confidence, so the interface always tells you how much of what you see is measured and how much is inferred. Hover any icon or ? for an explanation.

The areas

🧬Genome — raw DNA. Pick a file, fetch a region, translate it, find open reading frames. Real human chromosome 21 and the mitochondrial genome are included.
🧱Anatomy — the inventory. Counts coding, UTR, intron and intergenic bases, exons and introns, gene density and layout, CpG islands and repeats; compares mitochondrion, human and worm.
🧩Blocks — the map. Every delimited block of a chromosome drawn to scale and nested: domains (the genome's nodes between CTCF boundaries), genes with their transcripts and exons, curated repeats, ENCODE regulatory elements and the classified UNKNOWN space; click for details, search a gene, highlight one class, and in edit mode drag blocks to propose a new layout with plausibility checks.
🔁Flow — DNA to RNA to protein. One gene drawn as three linked lanes with its regulation above; hover to follow a base to its codon and residue; drop a single-base change and see what it does to the protein.
📝Program — biology as code. Genes, proteins and rules in BioLang; compile checks references and evidence; run draws the trajectory.
⏳Ageing — the timers. Telomere attrition, somatic mutations, epigenetic age and senescence for a chosen cell type and environment.
👥Twin — one person. Measured state (streamed from real data), variants in timer genes, forks and side-by-side futures.
🌱Space — development. A morphogen source patterns a field of cells; the segmentation clock lays down segments; germ layers form along a gradient.
🔍Debugger — why. Stop when a condition is met and read the rules and evidence behind the value.
🧫Molecules — RNA and proteins. Every isoform of a gene and where it is expressed (GTEx); our translation checked against UniProt; the AlphaFold structure with confidence per residue; the protein's compiled definition from seven public databases with evidence per section; its neighbourhood in the local knowledge graph; and any of its Reactome pathways run as a reachability graph with the protein knocked out.
🎗️Cancer — the same genome, changed. Compare a person's normal and tumour samples, or analyse a tumour alone (VEP on any chromosome, likely-germline variants set aside, mutant peptides, pathway reactions lost): consequences, driver frequencies and hotspots from 10,945 tumours, suggested cancer types, reachable surface targets, and a packet for a focused AI agent.
📊Progress — jobs running in the background with live progress, the project's task log, and the measurements obtained so far.
📚Libraries — the parts list. 45 reusable modules the genome contains, verified against curated databases.

A first walk-through

1Open Genome, keep the mitochondrial genome, press Translate: the real COX1 protein appears from the DNA.
2Open Program: the repressilator (a synthetic 3-gene oscillator) runs on arrival. Change max: 200 to max: 50 and press Run.
3Open Ageing, set mutagen × to 2 and press Simulate, then Compare with baseline.
4Open Twin, fork HG002 with TERT:nonsense and Run A against B.
5Open Debugger and press Run to breakpoint: it stops when LacI passes 30 and explains why, with sources.
6Open Anatomy: the mitochondrion, human chromosome 21 and worm chromosome III appear side by side. Hover a measure for what it means.
7Open Flow, keep APP and type 25897620 C>T in the variant box, press Trace: the base becomes codon 673 and p.Ala673Thr, the protective variant; press the residue button to see it on the structure.
8Open Molecules with TP53 and click a pathway chip: the pathway runs with p53 knocked out and the lost reactions turn red.
9Open Progress to watch background jobs fill in (whole-genome inventory, UNKNOWN classification, proteome coverage), and to read the task log.
Evidence pills: experimental measured in the lab · curated from a curated database · predicted from a model · inferred an estimate · none a placeholder. Confidence runs 0 to 1.
