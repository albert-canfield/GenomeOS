GenomeOS

🧭Start here

🧬
GenomeOS is an executable model of biology.DNA is the source, BioLang is the language you write biology in, and the runtime plays it forward through time. Every rule carries its evidence and a confidence, so the interface always tells you how much of what you see is measured and how much is inferred. Hover any icon or ? for an explanation.

The areas

🧬Genome — raw DNA. Pick a file, fetch a region, translate it, find open reading frames. Real human chromosome 21 and the mitochondrial genome are included.
🧱Anatomy — the inventory. Counts coding, UTR, intron and intergenic bases, exons and introns, gene density and layout, CpG islands and repeats; compares mitochondrion, human and worm.
📝Program — biology as code. Genes, proteins and rules in BioLang; compile checks references and evidence; run draws the trajectory.
⏳Ageing — the timers. Telomere attrition, somatic mutations, epigenetic age and senescence for a chosen cell type and environment.
👥Twin — one person. Measured state (streamed from real data), variants in timer genes, forks and side-by-side futures.
🌱Space — development. A morphogen source patterns a field of cells; the segmentation clock lays down segments; germ layers form along a gradient.
🔍Debugger — why. Stop when a condition is met and read the rules and evidence behind the value.
📚Libraries — the parts list. 45 reusable modules the genome contains, verified against curated databases.

A first walk-through

1Open Genome, keep the mitochondrial genome, press Translate: the real COX1 protein appears from the DNA.
2Open Program: the repressilator (a synthetic 3-gene oscillator) runs on arrival. Change max: 200 to max: 50 and press Run.
3Open Ageing, set mutagen × to 2 and press Simulate, then Compare with baseline.
4Open Twin, fork HG002 with TERT:nonsense and Run A against B.
5Open Debugger and press Run to breakpoint: it stops when LacI passes 30 and explains why, with sources.
Evidence pills: experimental measured in the lab · curated from a curated database · predicted from a model · inferred an estimate · none a placeholder. Confidence runs 0 to 1.
