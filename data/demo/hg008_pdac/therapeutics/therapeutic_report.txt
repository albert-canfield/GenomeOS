EXPERIMENTAL MECHANISMS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  immune_marker_delivery         no candidate satisfies its requirements
    tumour-selective delivery makes the cell display a strongly immunogenic surface marker, which pre-existing or adaptive antibodies then attack
    - experimental: the whole chain from selective delivery to marker display to effective immune attack has no clinical precedent that GenomeOS can cite
    - selectivity carries the entire safety argument, because any healthy cell that takes up the carrier is marked for destruction too
  rna_delivery                   no candidate satisfies its requirements
    a tumour-selective carrier delivers RNA that is translated inside the tumour cell
    - endosomal escape is the unsolved step and is not modelled here
    - lysosomal routing helps a cleavable payload and hurts RNA, which must escape before it
  tumour_suppressor_restoration  no candidate satisfies its requirements
    the inactivated gene's product is restored inside the tumour cell
    - Correcting or replacing one gene does not restore a normal cell. An established malignancy carries several cooperating drivers, passenger mutations, copy-number change, aneuploidy and epigenetic dysregulation; reverting one of them removes one dependency, not the disease.
    - restoration must reach every malignant cell to matter, which is a delivery problem no current carrier solves
  genome_editing                 no candidate satisfies its requirements
    the tumour allele is corrected or disrupted in place
    - Correcting or replacing one gene does not restore a normal cell. An established malignancy carries several cooperating drivers, passenger mutations, copy-number change, aneuploidy and epigenetic dysregulation; reverting one of them removes one dependency, not the disease.
    - editing must also reach the nucleus of every malignant cell; surface access is only the first of several barriers

REQUIRED MISSING DATA
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - tumour RNA-seq: no target's expression in this tumour can be established from DNA alone
  - HLA genotype: presentation is allele-specific; without it no mutant peptide can be prioritised
  - tumour purity and variant allele fraction: clonal and subclonal alterations cannot be separated, so an antigen on 10% of cells looks like one on 100%
  - copy number: amplification is one of the strongest reasons a surface target is over-displayed, and a homozygous deletion removes a target that a mutation list would still be offering
  - structural variants: a fusion creates a protein that exists in no healthy cell, which is the cleanest tumour specificity there is; a VCF of point mutations cannot show one
  - a cohort of the same cancer type: without one there is nothing to say how often this cancer type raises a gene above normal tissue, so selectivity falls back to a tissue-specificity class
  - tumour proteomics: RNA is not protein; abundance at the protein level is unmeasured
  - surface proteomics: the only direct measurement of what is actually displayed on the tumour cell surface
  - immunopeptidomics: the only evidence that a mutant peptide is genuinely presented rather than predicted
  - single-cell tumour data: heterogeneity and co-expression of two antigens in the same cell are otherwise unknowable

LIMITATIONS
==============================================================================

  - This analysis ran at data level 2 of 9. Conclusions that need a higher
    level are reported as unavailable, not estimated.
  - No tumour RNA or protein data was supplied, so for every candidate the
    chain is: DNA evidence present, RNA evidence unavailable, protein evidence
    unavailable, and surface expression cannot be established.
  - Localisation, topology, trafficking and healthy-tissue expression are
    curated population-level knowledge about the gene, not measurements of
    this patient's tumour.
  - A matched normal genome was supplied: every tumour record whose
    chromosome, position and alleles appear among the normal's calls was
    removed, and the rest are somatic by that subtraction rather than by
    population frequency. The match is exact, so a variant written differently
    in the two files is not removed.
  - Mechanism compatibility scores describe how well the biology fits a
    mechanism class. They are not probabilities that a patient responds.
  - Correcting or replacing a single gene does not restore a normal cell:
    established malignancies carry several cooperating alterations.
  - GenomeOS produces no therapeutic sequence, construct, vector, formulation
    or protocol.

GenomeOS generates computational research hypotheses. Therapeutic target rankings and mechanism suggestions are not clinical recommendations and require experimental and clinical validation.
